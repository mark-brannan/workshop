All four: Orion
Inches. Only the hull is solid: batteries rest on the floor or hull as low as they can and pass through everything else, so all four can always be placed. Whatever overlaps, or comes within 1/8, turns red and see-through, and the table says by how much. "On the slope" tilts about an athwartships axis (square to the side walls); "Rotate" then turns the battery within that slope. The shell numbers at the top of this file are best guesses from photos, not confirmed aboard; see README.md.
