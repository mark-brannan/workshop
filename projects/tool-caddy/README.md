Prototype, not designed. Numbers are owner-stated, not calipered here; zone spacings are "for now".

Behind B: S, wire snips nose down, two open arms each topped by a C hand (10 dia max) that takes a shoulder (41 wide, 47 from the tip); and W, strippers nose down in a small tower with a 6 thick slot, 20 wide at the base widening to 30 at the top, 50 deep (handles stand ~120 above, 60 wide, 12 thick). Both clear of C and E. Back row: F (thin drivers, 45 deg) behind the tray; C (9 long drivers) with its footboard just behind the caliper beam; V, a vase box for rulers and long tools, on the right in the space left. Base 414 x 192.

C supports: a half-circle cradle (13 dia) at 30 along the driver, and a U-slot clip for the 3 mm shaft (open at the top, 4 above the axis) at 91 along the driver, above the footboard. The cradle is narrower than the 18 handles; confirm what it holds.

Caliper outline traced from the vendor photo (9.45" long, jaw 3.07"); the jaw lies 45 mm to the front of the beam and 31 mm behind.

## Open

- Caliper jaw side (front vs back of the beam) is my pick from the photo's orientation; a different pose is a turn, not a mirror.
- F: footprint is ~85 deep from the footboard to the tip's reach, plus the swivel's radius.
- F: all Ø12 swivels at 6 c-c collide unless they alternate ends or stagger in height. Confirm how they stand, the shaft diameter and how many.
- Post and shaft fit allowances (printed holes run undersize; gauge first).
