# Tool caddy

Symphony: none yet

Purpose: a small desktop caddy holding the bench's small drivers, in three zones.

Prototype, not designed. Numbers are owner-stated, not calipered here; zone spacings are "for now".

## Zones

| Zone | Holds | Count | Size | Pitch | Orientation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A: precision drivers | very small screwdrivers | open tray, no slots | 85 long x 50 wide, flat and open for now | n/a | lying flat |
| B: hex socket drivers | small hex socket drivers | 7 | 110 tall/long | 15 c-c | handle down, on a post |
| C: long drivers | assorted screwdrivers | ~9 | ~155 long | 20 c-c | tip down |

## Zone B: post

| Item | Value | Note |
